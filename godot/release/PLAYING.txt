Tattletale 2-Pack — Pen Lucy / Windows release candidate

START
Extract the complete ZIP to a folder, then open Tattletale-PenLucy.exe.
Keep Tattletale-PenLucy.pck beside the executable. Godot Editor, Python,
Blender, and the source checkout are not needed to play. This candidate
targets Windows x86_64 and the Forward+ Vulkan renderer.

This is an unsigned release candidate, not a signed store release.

CONTROLS
WASD: move. Shift: run. Space: jump. Mouse: look.
Left/right arrows: turn. V: first/third person. E: interact.
Hold right mouse: orbit the camera.
Esc: settings/resume. M: neighborhood map.
Map: drag to pan; wheel or +/- to zoom; Find me centers the player.
Use the legend for place symbols; hover or select a symbol for its name.
Conversations: E/Enter/Space to continue; arrows to choose; Esc to leave.
This build focuses on helping neighbors, documenting cases and reporting what
you witnessed. Player attacks, car driving and alignment scoring are disabled.

BICYCLE
F near the bicycle: ride or pick it up. F while riding slowly: get off.
W: pedal. S: brake/reverse. A/D: steer. Space: hop. Shift: pedal harder.
Camera orbit does not steer. Slow down for curbs, people and traffic.
After a crash, wait for the rider to recover before riding or saving again.

BASKETBALL
E near a court ball: pick it up. Hold left mouse to charge; release to throw.

PHONE
P: open/put away the phone. 1: Contacts. 2: Camera. 3: Photos.
4: Cases. 5: Police. Click app controls or use Tab/arrows and Enter.
Camera: left mouse/Enter takes a photo; wheel zooms.
Photos stay in the phone gallery. Cases shows recorded progress and evidence.
Police: Enter or the call button reports a witnessed incident or asks officers
to check the area. A report is not proof of who committed an offense.
Esc/Backspace: back. F5 while the phone is open: save progress.
M opens the map; F6 opens inspection. Put the phone away before riding.

FREE-FLY INSPECTOR — PRESERVED IN THIS BUILD
F6 during play: enter/leave inspector.
WASD: fly. E/Space: rise. Q: descend. Shift: boost. Ctrl: precision.
Wheel: speed. Esc: release mouse. Click: capture mouse again.
F7: copy location. F8: screenshot. M: inspection map.

SAVING
Settings save immediately. Progress autosaves on stable ground every 30 seconds.
Get off the bicycle, finish any crash/impact recovery, and leave an active
confrontation before using Save progress in settings. The game explains when
a checkpoint must wait. Case and incident records are included in checkpoints.
The preceding checkpoint is retained as a backup. Keep your save files when
updating the executable/PCK. Older supported saves load through the game's
compatibility path; exporting a build does not reset the saved game.
Default Windows save folder:
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Tattletale 2-Pack\
