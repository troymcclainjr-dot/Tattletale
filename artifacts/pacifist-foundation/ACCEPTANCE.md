## Live-world performance sample

[liveworld-1080p/metrics.json](C:/files/projects/Tattletale/.work/pacifist-foundation/liveworld-1080p/metrics.json) reports **`valid_active_play=true`**, **`measurement_issues=[]`**, zero stalled movement windows, and no timeline anomalies. The world clock and simulation advanced; no world/logic hiding or population-disabling diagnostics were enabled.

Measured configuration: Ryzen 9 5950X / RTX 3070 Ti, Godot 4.7.2 debug build, Vulkan Forward+, Balanced quality, Volumetric clouds, private 1920×1080 viewport at scale 1, uncapped FPS and VSync off. This is a short instrumented route sample, not a sustained-session or release-binary benchmark.

The `traffic` bucket includes other grouped world services; it is not a measurement of cars alone. GPU/render timing and CPU phases overlap and must not be added into a synthetic frame total. No causal before/after speedup is claimed, and this 10-second sample does not establish spontaneous incident frequency or every traversal condition.
