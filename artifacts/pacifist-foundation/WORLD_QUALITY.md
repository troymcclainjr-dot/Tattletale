## Reference basis and regeneration

Existing architecture research cites [Maryland Historical Trust Waverly B-5145](https://apps.mht.maryland.gov/Medusa/PDF/BaltimoreCity/B-5145.pdf), [Baltimore City Design Manual](https://planning.baltimorecity.gov/sites/default/files/TransForm%20Design%20Manual-%20Final%20_2019%20Update%20-%20No%20Mayor%20Name.pdf) and [10–16 East Chase Street](https://apps.mht.maryland.gov/nr/NRDetail.aspx?COUNTY=Baltimore+City&FROM=NRCountyList.aspx&NRID=596). These informed porch/bay/cornice families and row coherence; Waverly/manual access was limited to indexed descriptions, not exhaustive PDF/photo review. Vegetation research cites [TreeBaltimore approved trees](https://www.treebaltimore.org/approved-street-tree-list), [UMD Eastern Redcedar](https://extension.umd.edu/resource/eastern-redcedar), [UMD privacy screens](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/plants-mixed-privacy-screens) and [Maine Eastern White Pine](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mfs/publications/handbooks_guides/forest_trees/pdf/eastern_white_pine.pdf). These guide stylized habits, not botanical identification of surveyed plants.

Architecture regeneration uses the staged/selective source publication and GLB merge in its report; never publish regenerated terrain/placements as a shortcut. Vegetation uses `blender/city/update_vegetation_templates.py`, preserving records and growth binding. Props rebuild runtime shared templates/cache on launch. Root owns all engine/export runs and final graph refresh.
