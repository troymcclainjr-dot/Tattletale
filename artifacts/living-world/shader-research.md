# Low-cost visual improvements for the Pen Lucy Godot city

Follow-up: [implemented changes and decisions](../render-audit/shader-implementation.md), [measured rendering audit](../render-audit/acceptance.md).

Researched 2026-10-30 against the official Godot 4.x documentation and the current project source. The project declares Godot 4.7/Forward+ in `godot/project.godot`; the renderer table below also calls out Mobile and Compatibility limits. **Costs are architectural estimates, not FPS measurements.** Verify in the installed 4.7.2 build with matched camera paths, resolution, time of day, render scale, quality, and warm caches.

## Existing render budget and style

- `city_vegetation.gd` merges 1,780 trees and 324 bushes from seven near/far templates into one surface per resident 48 m cell. Near trees cast shadows; far trees do not. The streamer retains a 400 m tree horizon. The new wind path adds one custom vec4 per merged vertex and one shared shader, with no extra draw, texture, screen pass, or per-tree tick. Its maximum tip offset is about 6.5 cm, and its per-vertex weight holds lower trunks and all bushes still.
- `city_render_optimizer.gd` batches buildings into 150 m chunks, generates mesh LODs and position-only shadow meshes, then controls shadow splits/distance and SSAO by quality. `city_streaming.gd` changes residency distances. `city_settings.gd` already exposes Low/Balanced/High and render scale. Avoid bypassing those controls with unbounded shader detail.
- `city_surface.gdshader` has world-scale brick, concrete, asphalt, facade, and earth wear with derivative-aware edges. `city_road_wear.gdshader` handles markings and potholes; `city_glass.gdshader` uses restrained dusty reflections. `clay.gdshader` is now a clean shared PBR material despite its legacy name. Preserve authored colors and geometry. Do not add full-screen grain, thumbprint/clay normals, generic noise on every material, or global color crushing.
- `pen_lucy_level.gd` sets filmic tonemapping, a procedural sky, light depth fog, directional sun/fill, and SSAO at radius 1.5/intensity 1.3. The low preset disables SSAO. A separate day/night owner should adjust these resources through the existing quality controls; the vegetation provider only accepts simulation seconds for wind.

| 4 | **Shadow distance and LOD tuning** in `city_render_optimizer.set_quality()` and `city_vegetation.set_presence()`: compare current 55/90/120 m sun shadows, 2/4 splits, and near/far tree thresholds. | Better nearby contact and less distant shimmer, potentially performance gain. | Existing controls. Shadow map fill and winded near-tree shadow vertices are the principal costs. The default far-tree shadow-off policy is sensible. | 5 | **SSAO restraint or removal by preset**, in `city_render_optimizer.set_quality()` and the environment. Compare 1.3 intensity against subtler values and Half Size/quality settings rather than layering SSIL. | Cleaner object contact without dark halos. | Full-screen SSAO is a measurable GPU pass. Half Size is faster but may lose small details. Existing Low disables it. | Forward+ yes; Mobile no; Compatibility has simplified SSAO since Godot 4.6 with different appearance. |
| 6 | **Selective street decals** only for a handful of high-value story/landmark marks, after checking existing `city_surface` and `city_road_wear` coverage. | Local narrative detail rather than procedural repetition. | Extra projected texture and clustered element; not free on a city-sized scene. On Mobile only eight decals can affect a mesh, so large batched building meshes may hit the limit. The existing geometry/wear approach is preferable for repeated roads. | Forward+/Mobile yes; Compatibility no. Use mesh marks or existing wear shader for Compatibility. |
| 7 | **Rare wetness state**, scoped to street/asphalt/curb materials and driven by explicit weather state, not a universal shiny overlay. Needs dry/wet art direction and rain logic first. | Material roughness in all; SSR only Forward+. Mobile/Compatibility need opaque material response or static reflection probes. |
| 8 | **Authored detail normals/AO** only for close brick/trim/ground assets where modeled relief is insufficient. Bake AO into authored vertex colors or texture only if it adds contact absent from existing geometry. | More surface relief at close range. | Texture bandwidth, tangent data, potential aliasing and material variants; repeated procedural micro-noise in `city_surface` is a poor trade. No blanket pass. | Normal/AO material inputs broadly supported; SSAO/SSIL renderer limits above. |

Do **not** prioritize SDFGI, SSIL, volumetric fog, broad SSR, global grain, or alpha-blended foliage for the current low-cost goal. Godot documents SDFGI as demanding and Forward+ only; SSIL and volumetric fog are also Forward+ only. Transparent overlap adds fill and sorting cost. The current opaque, solid-color foliage can receive shadows and avoids that overhead.

## Wind implementation and validation contract

`CUSTOM0.xy` stores the per-tree anchor in cell-local XZ, `CUSTOM0.w` stores a height-based sway weight. The rendering audit now uses the previously reserved `.z` for the squared height/radius ratio and performs equivalent inverse-transpose normal correction in the vertex shader; template wind weights are cached. A tree's world anchor determines its stable phase, independent of cell load order. The mask rises above normalized height 0.25 and is halved for far LOD; bushes get zero. The fragment path decodes the existing sRGB vertex colors and keeps roughness/specular equal to the prior StandardMaterial3D settings. Near shadow passes use the same deformed vertices; far shadows remain off. There is no `TIME` dependency because Godot's built-in `TIME` does not stop on pause. The root passes real simulation elapsed seconds through `set_wind_time()`; at 20 Hz, the maximum between-update displacement is about 1.6 mm at the chosen slow-wave speed. `set_wind_enabled(false)` and `--no-city-wind` keep the same mesh and shader for a movement A/B. `--no-city-wind-shader` instead uses the exact previous StandardMaterial3D settings on the same merged mesh/custom layout, permitting a material-cost A/B; `get_stats().wind_shader` identifies that run.

The `get_stats()` fields `wind_enabled`, `wind_time`, `wind_vertices`, and `estimated_raw_mesh_bytes` expose the scope. The byte value is a **CPU-side uncompressed array estimate** (position 12 + normal 12 + color 16 + custom0 16 = 56 bytes/vertex); it excludes indices, resources, allocations, GPU packing, shadows, and temporary merge buffers. The full dataset has 1,780 trees × 276 far or 1,116 near vertices, but the streamer keeps a subset resident. Do not infer GPU memory or FPS from those counts.

Validate the focused `city_vegetation_batch_smoke.gd` fixture on Godot 4.7.2, then run paired camera-path captures with `--no-city-wind` on/off in each intended renderer/quality. Check (1) no root motion/branch tearing, (2) material color parity against a prior build, (3) shadow synchronization, (4) no culling pop near frustum edges, (5) matched draw calls and residency, and (6) GPU frame time distributions after warmup. A 20 Hz uniform step should be visually smooth but must be checked in motion. The optional toggle is a quality escape hatch if GPU vertex time rises materially.

## Day/night and streetlamp implementation

`city_day_night.gd` samples the root `city_world_time.gd` clock. Its 09:00 sample exactly reproduces the existing sun color/energy, fill color/energy, sky colors, 0.52 ambient energy and 0.00055 fog density. Dawn and dusk are smooth curves; at night the sky remains navy and blue fill/ambient keep the walking route readable. It does not change SSAO or add volumetric fog, bloom, auto exposure, or a full-screen grade. It updates ambient/fog, physical directional lights and lens/nearby-lamp emission at 20 Hz, a separate shadowless sky-only sun plus sky colors/angle at most 2 Hz, and the nearest-lamp pool at 4 Hz. Identical clock values cause no light or sky writes. Godot documents that a changing directional light can force a sky radiance refresh; the Sky therefore uses incremental 128-pixel radiance processing and `sky_updates` counts actual sky-affecting write batches. This still needs GPU measurement.

The imported `furniture.glb` `Lamps` mesh has a separate glow material surface. Its first lamp's lens surface has measured center `(-24.000, 4.918, 0.319)` in original GLB world coordinates. `CityDressing.source_lamp_mesh()` subtracts `(-24, 0.16, -0.2)`, giving local lens center `(0, 4.758, 0.519)`. Authored and network `lamp_anchors` now report `yaw` and transformed `head_position`; the light pool uses only those actual heads. The controller duplicates that lens material once and changes its emission for every existing lamp mesh, including the network MultiMesh. It creates at most eight nearby, 11.5 m, shadowless OmniLight3D nodes. Godot's Mobile renderer limits each mesh to eight omnilights, so the pool deliberately stays at eight globally; the actual overlap may be lower. Light and sky frame times must be measured; neither 20 Hz interpolation nor the eight-light cap alone proves a low GPU cost.
