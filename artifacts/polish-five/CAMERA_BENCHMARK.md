# Camera timing correction for performance validation

The original `sustained-polish-five-baseline-351835653100` and `sustained-polish-five-measured-191912600000` captures are **camera-confounded, noncausal comparisons**. They retain functional acceptance value, but identical configuration did not produce identical camera workloads.

The fixture previously applied 0.3 pixels of look input per rendered frame when stationary or using the phone preview. It also carried the previous phase's camera heading into Greenmount. Different frame rates therefore produced different angular speed and views. Greenmount day ended at yaw -2.2758 versus -0.1474 radians; night ended at -9.6577 versus -7.7684 radians. Its mean draws rose 631 to 973 by day and 416 to 529 by night, while the other six phase means were approximately stable or lower. Frame time worsened in seven of eight phases, so the median/p95 change cannot be dismissed as only aggregate frame weighting; these captures still do not isolate its cause.

The benchmark-only correction uses absolute elapsed wall time at the old nominal 60 Hz rate (18 input pixels/second), aligns each walking phase with its route tangent, and gives each Greenmount phase a fixed zero heading. Production input, rendering, simulation, collisions, populations, phase durations, and phone actions are unchanged. The result records sweep schema 2 and each phase's initial/final yaw. Both baseline and candidate must be rerun using this same corrected fixture; neither corrected capture should be compared as matched evidence against the original captures.

Offline verification evaluates the actual pure GDScript sweep arithmetic and checks equal camera endpoints under 30/60/144/240 Hz and irregular/stalled frame schedules. It also verifies the nominal rate, nonnegative elapsed time, and fixture integration. This does not replace Godot parsing/runtime validation, which is owned by root. The frozen 600-second endurance capture is untouched.
